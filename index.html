Lean-To Greenhouse

The Lean-To Greenhouse is perfect for small gardens or urban spaces. Designed to be attached to an existing wall, it maximizes sunlight exposure while saving space. Its compact design ensures optimal growing conditions for vegetables, herbs, and flowers throughout the year.

Quonset (Hoop) greenhouse

A Quonset (Hoop) greenhouse is a tunnel-shaped structure made from metal or PVC hoops covered with plastic. It is easy to build, lightweight, and affordable. The curved design provides good wind resistance and even sunlight for plants. It is perfect for growing vegetables, flowers, and seedlings.

Gothic Arch greenhouse

The Gothic Arch Greenhouse features elegant pointed arches that provide increased headroom and superior snow-shedding capability. Its design maximizes interior space and sunlight exposure, making it perfect for growing taller plants and optimizing year-round productivity.

Geodesic Dome greenhouse

The Geodesic Dome Greenhouse features a strong, triangular framework that evenly distributes stress and maximizes structural stability. Its dome shape allows for optimal sunlight penetration and excellent air circulation, making it ideal for year-round growing and energy efficiency.
